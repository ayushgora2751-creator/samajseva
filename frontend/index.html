🎯 How to Play BINGO ARENA

1. Randomized 5×5 Board

Every player receives their own unique randomized grid containing numbers 1 to 25.

2. Take Turns Calling Numbers

On your turn, click any uncalled number on your board. That number is called and automatically stamped on all players' boards!

3. B - I - N - G - O Lines

Every completed horizontal row, vertical column, or diagonal line gives you 1 point. 1 line lights up 'B', 2 lines 'I', 3 lines 'N', 4 lines 'G', and 5 lines 'O'!

4. Winning the Match

The first player to reach 5 completed lines wins! The server validates all claims authoritatively.
